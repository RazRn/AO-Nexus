WHAT'S NEW IN 1.3.0 BETA 1

• Control Panel: sortable combat reports, individual pets, health/nano, attack targets, observed aggro, zones and client measurements.
• Reorder character cards and collapse/reorder Team, Damage Dumper and Timers. Your choices are remembered.
• Team presets keep login order and can keep the first client focused during startup.
• Confirmed logout/relog stops formation movement first. Cancel leaves the current formation unchanged.
• AO# plugin sets support shared selections under ALL, local installation compatibility and load diagnostics.
• Export Team, All and NPC statistics to clickable in-game scripts.
• Recommended settings are staged for review. Graphics, auto-minimize and experimental choices are preserved.

Automatic teams, formations, sync refresh, auto regroup, nearby reports and AO-VK management remain opt-in experiments. Minimized clients may stop drawing; the card tooltip explains why a preview can pause.

Known issue: an Engineer can repeat the standing-required casting message after login even when visually standing. This is not yet fixed. Updated game helpers require a client relog. See the Beta notes and guide for compatibility limits. Keep Defender enabled.
