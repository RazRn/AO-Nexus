WHAT'S NEW IN 1.2.0

• Game GUI redesigned around clear tasks. Layouts walks through a saved setup, target characters and the parts to replace. Less common actions are under More actions.
• Skins starts with the installation and shared accounts. Adding a skin and activating it are separate, clearly labelled steps.
• Scripts has separate Script files and Character macros views. Preview files before copying; macro copies have their own destination selection and explain what is replaced.
• Backups can be filtered by type. Each restore shows the original target, date and affected settings before you continue.
• Faster classic-client startup and team logins using Nexus's integrated launch engine. Each character and the whole team's loading time appear in the activity log.
• AOQuickLauncher is no longer required. The integrated engine gives Nexus direct control of startup and login scheduling; inherited work remains credited.
• Optional AOSharp responsiveness in the header and tray. Measurement components are included; enable Nexus Health Monitor in your AOSharp profiles. Stale measurements are distinguished from actual update pauses or missing IPC replies, with reasons and timings in the log.
• Optional client exit reports collect recent measurements and matching Windows errors in a ZIP you can save and share. An exit alone does not prove a crash. Nothing is uploaded automatically.
• Existing users get a short review of new options. Choices remain available in Settings and the setup wizard.
• Optional background minimization switches only between managed AO windows. Switching to a browser or another app does not minimize your active AO client.
• Dark opening of windows and dialogs, a smoother header activity line, version-linked changelog and a GitHub link under Settings → Updates.

Monitoring checks the local AOSharp update loop and its test IPC path. It does not measure game-server packet loss or guarantee delivery for other plugins.
