WHAT'S NEW IN 1.2.1

• Search your characters without losing the current selection. The selected total still includes characters hidden by the search.
• Login and team logout each have a “Don't show this confirmation again” checkbox. Turn either confirmation back on independently in Settings. Cancel never saves the checkbox.
• Client monitoring now starts with each character's status, a plain explanation and a suggested next step. Open Technical details when you need the measurements.
• Missing measurements, a client that is not responding and an exited client remain separate states. An exit alone does not prove a crash.
• The updated monitoring plugin flags recent nano casts while falling, with a character name, grouped reports and a next step. Action issues are separate from responsiveness. Reload the updated plugin through AOSharp to enable this.

Account limits, password choices and safe client matching still apply when confirmations are turned off. Monitoring remains optional and requires Nexus Health Monitor in the relevant AOSharp profiles. Nothing is uploaded automatically.
