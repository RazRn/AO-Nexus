CONTROL PANEL COMBAT REPORT SCRIPTS

Control Panel -> Export scripts... captures Team, All and NPC statistics.
Choose the installation's scripts destination and Save scripts. The exporter
discovers Prefs for running installations before offering saved account folders,
which can still refer to another installation. Choose Prefs... is the fallback.
Scripts live beside Prefs and are shared by characters in that installation.
No plugin reload, client restart or extra AO# plugin is needed.

In game:
  /nexus                  local command list
  /nexus-team             local list of Team reports
  /nexus-all              local list of nearby player reports
  /nexus-npc              local list of NPC reports

Post commands (use the active chat channel, do not change it):
  /nexus-team-dmg          total damage, includes nano and pets
  /nexus-team-nano         nano damage
  /nexus-team-pets         pet damage with individual named pets under owners
  /nexus-team-healing      healing
  /nexus-team-xp           XP
  /nexus-team-axp          alien XP
  /nexus-team-sk           shadowknowledge
  /nexus-all-dmg           nearby player damage, including tracked pets
  /nexus-all-nano          nearby nano damage
  /nexus-all-pets          nearby named pets grouped under owners
  /nexus-all-healing       nearby healing
  /nexus-npc-dealt         damage dealt by monsters
  /nexus-npc-taken         damage dealt TO monsters
  /nexus-npc-nano          monster nano damage
  /nexus-npc-healing       monster healing

Append -preview to any post command for private /text output, for example:
  /nexus-team-dmg-preview

Each post contains self-contained clickable text:// pages. Recipients do not
need Nexus, AO#, local scripts or an external website. Cyan identifies the
report, gold the row total, green the ranked owners and violet their pets.
Totals are ranked descending. Rates show /s, /m and /h. A snapshot timestamp,
measurement coverage and scope distinguish the saved report from live data.
Names cannot inject markup, chat commands or AO script substitutions.

Whole rows are paginated within 1000 bytes per chat link. At most five links
are posted by one command, with two seconds between links. Larger reports get
continuation commands such as /nexus-all-pets-2, listed in /nexus and the export
dialog. Numbered page labels identify the page within the complete report.
This is multiple independent clickable pages, not a website with nested links.

Refresh snapshot then Save scripts after a fight. Opening the export dialog
also captures a new snapshot. Exporting does not post or reset anything.
Existing scripts with the generated names are backed up through the regular
Nexus Scripts / Macros backup system; a failed batch write rolls back.
Earlier continuation files may remain on disk, timestamped with their original
snapshot; /nexus lists only the newly saved report suite.

Team follows the selected Control Panel character/preset filter. All and NPC
use their selected single observer, include nearby observed events only and
reset on zoning. XP/AXP/SK require native local measurements and are therefore
only offered for Team. Unavailable measurements are labelled, not zero-filled.
Row totals and the independently reset Control Panel total are labelled
separately. DMG/m in the Panel total is the average across measured owners,
matching Control Panel. Individual rates use the existing session clocks.
Pet rows are breakdowns, never added again to the owner/team total. Same-name
pet respawns under one owner are combined, with no pet IDs in the report.
