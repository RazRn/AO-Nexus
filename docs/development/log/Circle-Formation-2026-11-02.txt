Circle formation (experimental) - 2026-11-02

Control Panel > Team > Formation: Circle. The selected leader is at the centre;
the actual follower count divides the full circumference into equal angles.
Radius is configurable from 1 to 10 m (default 3 m), persisted separately from
Follow spacing. World-aligned slots avoid orbiting when the leader turns.
The circle moves with the leader's position using ordinary movement. Existing
combat, zoning, roster, line-of-sight, distance, progress and lease checks remain.
Stop restores the prior SyncManager movement setting. Attack sync is unchanged.
Older helpers must explicitly advertise CircleSupported before Start is allowed.
