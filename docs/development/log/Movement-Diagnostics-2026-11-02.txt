MOVEMENT SEPARATION INVESTIGATION - 2026-11-02

The selected AOSP Manager.Sync receiver ignores movement while active/zoning,
in another playfield, when the sender is not visible, or when the received
position is farther than 2 * (1 + follower RunSpeed / 100). Accepted messages
assign position/rotation and movement action. Increasing that limit is not
part of this change.

Nexus Health Monitor now samples positions, RunSpeed, playfield, visible
players and the supported ManagerSync.ManagerSync public settings on the
game thread every 0.5 seconds. It records the last outbound move timestamp
and the plugin's last accepted sender identity. It does not install or replace
IPC callbacks. Unsupported/absent plugins remain explicit.

Control Panel compares followers with the most recently viewed client.
Warnings mean a measured distance/channel/visibility condition, not proof
that any particular IPC packet was lost. Independently sampled positions can
differ in time, so the distance is diagnostic, not a delivery acknowledgement.
Snapshots older than three seconds, mismatched identities and invalid
coordinates cannot authorize regroup. The opt-in development recorder keeps
the bounded snapshots alongside update gaps and minimized state.

Recover/context menu offers Regroup with viewed character. Nexus confirms
the requested follower/leader, checks process identity and queues a command.
Nexus Shortcuts validates again on the game thread: same team and channel,
visible player, alive, not attacking/casting/zoning, within 60 units and five
vertical units, clear line of sight, no active formation/navigation. It uses
the game's normal follow request. The queue acknowledgement does not mean
follow succeeded; the in-game chat reports the validation result.

Loading requires restarting Nexus and loading the updated Health Monitor and
Nexus Shortcuts inside each game client. Existing loaded assemblies remain
old until restarted. Do not reset plugin preferences or change sync limits.
