The classic AnarchyOnline.exe has no IMAGE_FILE_LARGE_ADDRESS_AWARE flag.
On 64-bit Windows, setting it raises the 32-bit process's address-space limit
from 2 GB to 4 GB. This is per process, not a promise to fix every allocation
failure or a requirement to allocate 4 GB of physical memory.
