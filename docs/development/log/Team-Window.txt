AO NEXUS CONTROL PANEL

Open Control Panel at the right of the navigation bar. This is a detachable view of clients
started and tracked by Nexus. Choose a saved team or all running characters.
Click a card (or focus it and press Enter) to switch to that game window.
Live previews can be disabled for compact cards; Always on top is optional.
Control Panel choices are saved automatically in AONexus.json: previews, always-on-top,
team filter, active tab, observer, window size/position/maximized state, and divider.
Each Team/All/NPC tab remembers its metric, sorting, column widths and collapsed pets.
The observer is saved by character identity and reconnects after relog; an offline
observer stays explicitly selected. Combat script destination/report choices are
remembered too. Changes are debounced and flushed on close; telemetry refreshes do
not write settings. Existing profiles keep the previous defaults until changed.
These are view preferences, not saved combat counters or pending logout/relog actions.

Client health monitoring must be enabled. Health/nano and activity require
the updated Nexus Health Monitor plugin; restart existing game clients through
this build. No AO-VK requirement for the Team Window. Classic AOSharp windows
and the existing AO-VK opt-out remain available.

Cards show health/nano, responsiveness, attacking/idle, fighting target,
client CPU and memory, and the observed team leader.
Precise attack/cast progress bars are not implemented. Unknown, stale or zoning
vitals are blank; no values are guessed. Local reply time is not server ping.

Stats are sampled on the game update thread up to five times per second,
published through the existing bounded local snapshot twice per second, and
read through the existing monitor. No new listener or injection mechanism.
The view sends no combat commands or broadcast input. DWM supplies previews;
minimized-client previews pause, and offscreen tiles hide their thumbnails.

Validation: fixture data checks, two-language layouts, filtering, stale and
identity handling, DWM registration/properties/release, existing monitor and
snapshot tests. Live AO previews and multi-client gameplay still need user
acceptance; fixture screenshots contain simulated character data.

Pet breakdowns and team recovery - 2026-11-01
Team and All show expandable pet rows beneath their owners. Names update from
the current pet roster, including renames. Same-name pets under one owner are
combined across summons and sorted by name; IDs remain internal. Pet rows are
a breakdown of owner totals, so the footer does not count their damage twice.
All uses one observer's events and local rosters only for names/ownership.
Automatic observer selection moves to another client after its process exits;
temporary zoning does not silently switch the measurement source.

With updated Nexus Shortcuts loaded, Nexus remembers observed teams while it
runs. A member's process exit/replacement arms bounded recovery. The actual
remaining leader invites returning members; after the roster has rejoined,
leadership is restored to the prior leader. Ordinary manual team changes do
not arm recovery. Raids and teams containing unmanaged outsiders are excluded.
Full relog also forms a matching automatic saved team even when no preset is
selected in the main window. If no actual team was observed, its first saved
character leads. Nexus does not automatically restart a crashed game process.
Recovery needs the clients to return nearby, with their helpers loaded. It
keeps pending recovery for up to ten minutes, with a five-minute action window
and bounded invite/transfer retries. Recovery memory ends when Nexus closes.

Control Panel sections (2026-11-01)
Damage Dumper, Team and Timers each have a collapsible header. Click a header
for a compact bar; drag it onto another header or use its up/down arrows to
reorder. Drag the bottom edge of an expanded section to resize it. Order,
collapsed state, expanded height and the timers server selection are saved.
The old divider height migrates to Damage Dumper on first use. Timers starts
collapsed and shares the existing timer store and editor with the Timers window.
Collapsing Team hides DWM previews; collecting combat/health data continues.
Validation: 328 TeamWindow checks, including persistence, invalid saved layouts,
all-collapsed geometry, inline timers and existing timer UI behavior. Defender
custom scan of the Release executable returned no threats.
