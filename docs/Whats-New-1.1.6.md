# AO Nexus v1.1.6

- A four-step first-run wizard helps choose language, news/health servers, update checks, Windows startup and background notifications, then optionally adds accounts. Passwords are requested at login.
- Fresh setup holds news, health and GitHub connections until completion. Skip leaves optional online features off. Preferences and staged accounts are saved together on Finish.
- Existing/imported profiles keep their settings. **Settings → General → Run setup again…** revisits setup; Cancel discards changes.
- The Skins destination selector is now labelled **Target installation** and sits above the apply buttons. It identifies the accounts sharing that installation and still controls its skin list and download/import destination.
- Section headings use consistent typography; legend backgrounds blend with their surroundings and Settings actions have consistent spacing.
- The player guide and annotated screenshots have been refreshed.

Experimental same-account parallel login changes are not included.
